Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Xmas Daily handles information when you use the app.

Effective: October 30, 2026Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Who we are

Xmas Daily is provided by Minh Nguyen (“we”, “us”, or “our”). For privacy questions, contact us at mailforwork.minh@gmail.com.

2. Information stored in the app

Xmas Daily stores quiz progress and preferences locally on your device so the app can remember your journey, completed days, answers, language choice, and related settings. Depending on your Android backup and device-transfer settings, some app data may also be included in operating-system backup or transfer services.

We do not require you to create an account, and the app does not use a proprietary account database for this local progress in the current version. If this changes, we will update this policy.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads/AdMob to display App Open, banner, and interstitial advertising. These services may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, app/device information, diagnostics, and ad interaction information as needed to serve, measure, limit, and prevent fraud in advertising.

Google’s services may use cookies, mobile identifiers, or similar technologies according to their own policies and configuration. We do not control the data practices of third-party providers. Review their policies for details:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent before requesting advertising. Your choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized ads are shown.

You can manage available privacy choices through the consent or privacy controls presented by the app. Advertising availability may be limited if you decline consent or if an ad request is unavailable.

5. How we use information

  • To provide and remember quiz progress and app preferences.
  • To display and measure advertising through third-party ad services.
  • To maintain app security, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and improve reliability.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Sharing

Local quiz progress remains on your device in the current version. Information processed by advertising and consent providers may be transmitted to those providers or their service partners as necessary to provide their services, comply with law, measure advertising, and prevent fraud.

7. Retention and deletion

Local quiz progress can be removed by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app, subject to your device’s operating-system behavior. We do not currently provide an account deletion workflow because the current app does not require an account.

If we introduce accounts, server-side profiles, or other persistent online data, we will update this section and provide an appropriate deletion request mechanism.

8. Children’s privacy

Xmas Daily is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If the app is marketed to children or families, the publisher must review Google Play’s Families Policy, configure the app and ad SDKs accordingly, and update this policy before release.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information handled by the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and third-party providers maintain their own security controls.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. We will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel.

11. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: mailforwork.minh@gmail.com.
